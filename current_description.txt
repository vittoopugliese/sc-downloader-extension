Download SoundCloud Likes, playlists and tracks for free. Get access to the new Pitcher and Looper inside the player tools!

Your SoundCloud favorites should not be trapped in one tab. Open your Likes, a playlist, set, album, or a creator’s public Tracks page; choose the tracks you want or download them all; then save them directly to a folder you choose.

Downloads keep running in the background. Close the popup, switch tabs, or keep browsing SoundCloud while your collection is saved one track at a time.
