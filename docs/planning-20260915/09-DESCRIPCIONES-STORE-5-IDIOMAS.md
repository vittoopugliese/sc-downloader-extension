## EN — English: descripción base

Download SoundCloud Likes, playlists and tracks for free. Save your music to a folder you choose, and explore playback speed and A–B looping with Player Tools.

Your SoundCloud favorites do not have to stay in one tab. Open your Likes, a playlist, set, album, or a creator’s public Tracks page. Choose the tracks you want, select a preset amount, or download them all.

Downloads keep running in the background. Close the popup, switch tabs, or keep browsing SoundCloud while your collection is saved one track at a time.

• Download individual tracks, Likes, playlists, sets, albums and public creator uploads.
• Select individual tracks, a preset amount, or All.
• Start downloads from the extension popup or supported SoundCloud page controls.
• Save collections as numbered files and choose your download folder.
• Pause, resume or cancel bulk downloads.
• Follow progress in the extension badge and receive a notification when the job finishes.
• Use the artist’s original file when SoundCloud makes it available.
• Download most public tracks without an account, when SoundCloud permits it.

Player Tools adds playback controls inside SoundCloud: change speed, use Keep Tone where supported, and repeat an A–B section. Availability, trial limits and Pro features are explained in the extension. Original track and collection downloads remain free; transformed audio and loop exports are separate Player Tools features.

Audio quality depends on the source available from SoundCloud. Converting a stream to WAV does not restore the quality of an unavailable original.

Download only music you own or have permission to save. Respect artists’ rights and SoundCloud’s Terms of Service. This is an independent extension, not affiliated with or endorsed by SoundCloud.

### EN — Añadir al publicar la oferta Pro y Explorer

Player Tools Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase for the published Pro features, including loop WAV export and full-track export with supported playback transformations. Likes Explorer helps you rediscover older Likes in your library. Check the extension for the current offer and access details.

### EN — Añadir únicamente cuando el candidato con EQ/export FX esté verificado

Shape your listening with Reverb, Delay and a parametric Pro EQ with up to eight points, high/low-pass filters and optional hard clipping. Export an A–B loop or the full track as WAV with your applied sound settings.
