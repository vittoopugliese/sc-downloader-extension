## Description (English)

SEIB is a stem extractor and a set of player tools that run in your browser.

STEM EXTRACTOR
Split up to 2 minutes of audio into vocals, drums, bass and other. Separation runs on your own device with an open-source model; your audio is never uploaded.
• Start from your own audio files, or from a loop you select while a track plays
• Mute, solo and mix each stem, then arrange them in a playlist with a channel rack, piano roll, per-channel FX, EQ and automation
• Play slices of any stem as drum pads
• Separations and projects are saved in this browser

PLAYER TOOLS ON YOUTUBE AND SOUNDCLOUD
A compact panel next to the player:
• Speed from 0.1× to 4×, with Keep Tone to change tempo without changing pitch
• A–B loops on a frequency-colored waveform
• 8 sampler pads and a piano mode, played from your keyboard
• Reverb, delay, flanger, phaser, bitcrusher and saturation
• Parametric EQ with a live spectrum
• Bypass to compare with the original in one click

LIKES EXPLORER (SoundCloud)
Index your SoundCloud likes privately in this browser, search them and jump back years without endless scrolling.

PRIVACY
No account. No ads. Audio analysis and stem separation happen on your device. The first separation downloads the model once; Chrome keeps it cached.

SEIB does not download videos or tracks from YouTube or SoundCloud. Please use it with music you own or have permission to use.

## Policy notes before submitting

- The account already has a circumvention takedown (SoundCloud Track Downloader, 2026-09). Keep the listing free of "download", "rip" or "save from YouTube" wording.
- Loops recorded on YouTube reach the Stem Extractor, and the stems page can export each stem and the project as WAV. A reviewer can read that as a way to save YouTube audio. Decide before submitting whether YouTube-sourced projects should keep WAV export.
- "YouTube" and "SoundCloud" appear only in the description, not in the name or icon.
