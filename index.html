Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every tool is free right now, with no account and no card.

Does it download SoundCloud tracks?

No. Player Tools works on the track playing in SoundCloud's own player. You can save your loops and recordings as WAV.

What happened to SoundCloud Track Downloader?

It was removed from the Chrome Web Store, so Chrome now flags it and may turn it off. Player Tools is the new extension from the same developer. It's a different product and doesn't download tracks.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Stem separation runs on your device, and your Likes index stays in your browser. This site has no analytics.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome on desktop, version 116 or newer. Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store, like Edge and Brave, can use it too.

Is this made by SoundCloud?

No. Player Tools is an independent extension. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by SoundCloud.
