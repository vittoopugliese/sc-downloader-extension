    Free downloads. Clear limits for the tools.

    What counts as a trial use?

    One new, completed and activated A–B range uses one of the 20 loops. Repeating it, adjusting its markers or pausing does not use another. Clearing it and creating a new range does.

    What happens after the trial?

    Your last granted loop continues for its session. Once the trial ends, the musical tools require Pro. Full-track downloads without Player Tools effects remain free.

    What does “free downloads” include?

    Full tracks and collections without Player Tools effects, in the formats available from the source. “Original” means no effects; it does not guarantee the artist’s original upload. WAV export does not improve the source quality.

    What can I download?

    Use the extension only for content you own or have permission to save. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, approved by or maintained by SoundCloud.

    What about privacy?

    Audio and downloads stay in your browser. This landing has no analytics. The extension sends minimal pseudonymous product events, never track titles, music URLs, emails or license keys.
