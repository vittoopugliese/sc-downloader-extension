    Straight answers.

    No blurred lines between the free downloader and the optional creative tool.

    What stays free?

    Every full-track download feature: individual tracks, playlists, albums, likes, artist uploads, selection, formats, folders, metadata, artwork and background batches. No account or Looper purchase is required for them.

    How does the free Looper tryout work?

    The planned tryout lets you set and hear one A–B loop on an individual track and successfully save that selected range as one WAV. After that export, Looper—not the downloader—requires an unlock.

    Is Looper Pro a subscription?

    No. The early-access plan is a single USD 2.99 purchase, not a recurring subscription. Checkout is not open yet; this page will not take payment until purchase and restoration are ready.

    Can I restore a Looper purchase?

    Yes. Purchase restoration is part of the release requirement and will be available before sales open. Reinstalling the free downloader never requires a purchase.

    What can I download?

    Use the extension only for content you own or have permission to save. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, approved by or maintained by SoundCloud.

    What about privacy?

    The current extension processes audio and downloads in your browser and its Chrome Web Store disclosure states that it does not collect or sell user data. This landing has no analytics or form submission. View the current Store disclosure.
