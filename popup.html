    How it works

    Download tracks you own or have permission to download.

    Download from SoundCloud

    Open a track, playlist, Likes, or a user's Tracks page. Use the Download button here or the download button on the SoundCloud page.

    Choose a collection

    For playlists, Likes, and user tracks, choose a preset or Select to pick individual tracks. Downloads keep running if you close this popup.

    Choose a folder

    Use the folder button to select where files are saved. Choose Downloads to return to Chrome's normal Downloads folder.

    Formats and quality

    Auto selects the available stream. Original is available only when the artist has enabled an original download. Source quality varies by track.

    Progress and errors

    The status shows progress and saved files. If a track cannot be read or saved, retry after reloading SoundCloud or choosing the folder again.

    The speed selector controls SoundCloud playback from 0.1x to 4x and shows the active speed beside the player controls.

    Open the selector

    Press the speedometer in the player controls. Use the white download icon inside the panel to download the entire current track at the effective speed and Keep Tone setting. The orange WAV button exports only the selected A-B range.

    Quick speeds

    Choose one of the six quick speeds. The two X buttons are custom presets: hold one to save the slider's current speed, then click it whenever you want to restore that exact value. Custom presets stay saved between SoundCloud sessions.

    Precise control

    Drag the slider anywhere from 0.1x to 4x in 0.025x steps. Double-click the slider to return to the normal 1x speed.

    Keep Tone

    Enable Keep Tone to change duration while keeping the original musical pitch. Leave it off to let pitch rise or fall with speed. The option is disabled when the browser cannot preserve pitch.

    Temporary bypass

    The single bypass icon temporarily disables the loop and plays at 1x without losing the selected range, speed, or Keep Tone setting. The logo turns gray while bypass is active; press the icon again to restore everything.

    Remembered and kept active

    Your selected speed and Keep Tone choice are stored locally, restored on later visits, and applied to the current player and newly loaded SoundCloud audio.

    Downloads and Looper

    Full-track downloads from the white panel button and A-B WAV exports use the effective speed. Keep Tone preserves pitch; with it off, duration and pitch change. Bypass exports at the normal 1x rate.

    Close and troubleshoot

    Click outside the panel or press Escape to close it. If playback or saving a preference fails, the panel shows a message; reload SoundCloud and try again.

    Looper

    Looper follows the track in SoundCloud's persistent player, even when you navigate or close the control panel. Set A/B from the panel or edit the same range on a compatible waveform; WAV export remains beside the waveform.

    How to use Looper

    Looper is free on compatible individual SoundCloud tracks. It repeats the A–B range you choose and saves it as WAV.

    1. Open an individual track

      Open the SoundCloud track you want to work with, then wait for it to load or start playing it.

    2. Turn on the loop

      Open the speedometer panel and use Set A and Set B at the live playhead, drag either handle on the Loop slider, or press the loop button before More actions on a compatible waveform.

    3. Set A and B

      The panel and waveform edit the same A–B range in real time. The panel shows milliseconds for short tracks and hours when needed; waveform arrow keys move a marker 100 ms, or 1 second with Shift.

    4. Zoom in for precise points

      Scroll up over the waveform to zoom toward the cursor, then scroll down to zoom back out. It makes small A–B adjustments much easier.

    5. Save the selected range

      Choose Download loop to save the A–B range as a WAV file.

    The loop is temporary and follows the current player track across SoundCloud navigation. Changing the playing track removes it. The shared bypass pauses both loop and playback effects without forgetting their settings.
